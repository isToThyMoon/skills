# Competitor Brief

Produce a complete competitor brief for the product the user names, in one go.

1. Ask the user for the product and up to five competitors. If they do not know the competitors, find them.
2. For each competitor, search the web and read at least eight sources: their homepage, pricing page, docs, changelog, two reviews, one forum thread and one news article. Take careful notes on positioning, pricing, target customer, key features, recent launches, weaknesses.
3. Build a feature matrix comparing all competitors across at least 20 features.
4. Build a pricing comparison across all tiers, normalised to monthly per-seat price.
5. Write a SWOT for the user's product against the field.
6. Write the brief: executive summary, the matrix, the pricing table, the SWOT, three strategic recommendations, and an appendix with every source.
7. Review the whole brief for accuracy: every claim must cite a source from step 2.
8. Turn the brief into a 10-slide deck outline.
9. Write a short Slack message announcing the brief.

Keep all notes in your head as you go; write the files only at the end. Be accurate. Do not invent numbers.
